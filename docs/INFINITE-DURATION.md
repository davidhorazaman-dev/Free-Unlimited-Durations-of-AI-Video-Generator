# Infinite Duration / Infinite Hours

The product uses unbounded timeline semantics.

There is no fixed application-level project duration such as 10 minutes, 60 minutes, or 24 hours. A project is an ordered stream of scenes.

One hour, ten hours, one hundred hours, one thousand hours, or longer projects are represented by continuing to append scenes and jobs.

This does not claim that a provider, computer, storage system, network, or account has literally infinite resources. It is the architecture for effectively unlimited project duration within available resources.
